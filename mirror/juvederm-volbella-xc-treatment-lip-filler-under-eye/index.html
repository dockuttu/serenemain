Natural lip filler in Barboursville

Hyaluronic-acid lip fillers add volume, sharpen the border and smooth fine lip lines. Because the gel is temporary and can be dissolved, you can start small and build over time.

Our Barboursville patients choose between a half syringe (0.5 mL) for a subtle refresh and a full syringe (1 mL) for more noticeable shape, and we use Juvéderm Volbella XC or Ultra XC depending on the look you want. Every lip treatment starts with a close look at your proportions, your smile and any asymmetry, and we invite you back for a follow-up so we can fine-tune if needed. Individual results vary.

After Your Visit

Aftercare

Love your results, protect your investment — a few simple steps help minimize swelling and bruising.

First 24 Hours

  • Avoid touching, rubbing, or massaging the area.
  • Apply ice packs gently for swelling (10 min on / off).
  • Stay hydrated and sleep with your head elevated.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise.
  • Avoid alcohol and excessive salt.
  • Avoid excessive heat — saunas, hot tubs, steam rooms, tanning beds.

First 48 Hours

  • Avoid facials, massage, microneedling, chemical peels, and laser treatments.
  • Avoid pressure on the area — face-down sleeping, tight goggles, excessive facial massage.

Lips Specifically

  • Expect more swelling the next morning.
  • Use a straw minimally for the first day.
  • Small lumps are often swelling and usually soften over 1–2 weeks.
